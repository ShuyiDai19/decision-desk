# Output of `npx deepspace integrations info`, with the hard-to-read lines reworded in plain English.

email/send
  Sends an email. Every email is sent from DeepSpace Apps <notifications@updates.app.space>; anything you put in `from` is ignored. To have replies go to a real person, put their address in `reply_to`. The body can be HTML or plain text, and cc and bcc are supported.
  billing: $0.013 per request
